Joined group — one unified field behind several containers

A single field is mounted on the group wrapper; the "cards" are translucent, so one continuous constellation flows behind all of them and through the gaps. Links should cross card boundaries seamlessly, and the pointer works across the whole group as one surface.

Windowed group — one unified field showing through separate containers

The inverse of the joined group: one field spans the whole wrapper, but a multi-subpath path() clip makes it visible only inside the three windows — the gaps show nothing. Watch a particle drift out of one window and re-appear in the next: it is the same continuous field passing behind the mask. The clip is rebuilt live on resize via setOptions.
