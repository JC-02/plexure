Content & exclusions

The canvas is always pointer-events: none and sits behind content, so it can never block clicks, selection, or focus. On top of that, anything marked data-plexure-ignore (dashed gold outline below) suppresses the hover reaction while the pointer is on it — for controls or content the field should defer to.
