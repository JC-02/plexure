plexure test harness

Manual visual-testing hub. Serve the repo root (npx http-server .) and browse. A viewport field runs behind this hub as a baseline smoke test.

1 · Full page

'page' target behind a real article: title, body text, filler content, long scroll.

 2 · Partial view / sections

Fields scoped to individual sections only, including a nested field inside a fielded section.

 3 · Basic containers

Single card, independent siblings (one reacts at a time), and a unified field behind a joined group.

 4 · Custom polygon shapes

Hexagons, stars, L/T-shapes, blobs, slivers — plus a shrinking size series to find the practical minimum.

 5 · Field configurations

Star sizes, colours, opacities, softness, drift, edge behaviours, seeds — plus live pause/resume/intensity controls.

 6/7 · Content & exclusions

Content above the field, interactive controls inside it, and data-plexure-ignore zones that suppress the hover reaction.

 8 · Sim-aware shape clipping

Particles that live inside a shape rather than being masked by it. Side-by-side against mask-only clipping, plus fit modes and a cursor stress test.

Baseline checks on this page

The hub itself runs a viewport-fixed field. Verify: pointer links follow the cursor anywhere on the page · switching tabs pauses it (check with the FPS meter in DevTools) · resizing reflows without a visible jump · text stays crisp above the canvas.
