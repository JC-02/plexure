Partial view — section scoping

Alternating sections. Only the marked ones carry a field, and each field must stay strictly inside its own section: no bleed above or below the border lines, and the pointer only reacts while inside that section.

Plain section (no field)

Nothing should render behind this text. If particles appear here, scoping is broken.

Fielded section A

Field in the section's own colour. Move the pointer across the top and bottom borders — the reaction must cut off exactly at the section edge (pointerenter / pointerleave, not distance).

Plain section again

Empty. The two neighbouring fields must not react while the pointer is here, even within their cursor radius of the border.

Fielded section B — with a nested field

This section has its own field and contains a card with a second, independent field. Hovering the card must deactivate the section field (innermost claim wins); leaving the card hands the pointer back to the section.

Nested field

Hover here: only this card reacts. The section field around it goes inert.
