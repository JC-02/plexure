Sim-aware shape clipping

A clipTo string or bare Path2D masks the render alone. The simulation keeps running in the full bounding box, so particles drift behind the mask and vanish. Pass a { path } object instead and the simulation itself is confined: the engine seeds particles inside the shape and re-places them inside when they leave.

The distinction, side by side

Same shape, same seed, same count. Watch the edges. On the left, particles stream out of the shape and disappear at the mask boundary, and the interior thins and refills unevenly. On the right, every particle lives inside the shape for good.

fit

Maps the shape's own coordinate space into the container. All three use the same 100×100 star path in a wide box. contain fits it whole, cover fills the box and overflows, none uses raw coordinates.

Shapes worth stressing

Concave, hollow, and thin. The ring must keep its hole empty. The sliver covers about 5% of its container, which is the case that makes naive rejection sampling fall over.

edgeBehaviour inside a shape

An arbitrary shape has no opposite edge to cross to, so wrap and respawn both re-place particles inside. fade ramps a particle out as it nears the boundary. That one costs two to three extra containment tests per particle per frame.

Cursor stress

Judgement call needed. Drag hard and fast across the shape. The cursor pull can shove many particles out at once, and the engine re-places each one somewhere else, which may read as popping. If it looks bad, clamping particles at the boundary instead costs a small change. Use the buttons to compare a gentle pull against an aggressive one.
