Custom polygon shapes

Every shape below is a normal element with a CSS clip-path, passed to the field as clipTo so the canvas clips to the same shape. The simulation still runs in the bounding box (sim-aware clipping is the v0.2 feature) — what you are testing here is that the visual clip is exact, corners and concave edges included, and that the pointer reaction works inside the visible shape.

Geometric

Unorthodox — concave corners, notches, extremes

Slivers and extremes

Degenerate aspect ratios. Watch for particles bunching, links spanning the short axis wall-to-wall, and the cursor pull overwhelming the space.

Minimum size series

Same default config at shrinking sizes. The count floor (minCount: 24) means small boxes get proportionally denser — judge where it stops looking good. That observed threshold becomes our documented minimum recommendation, and below whatever you pick we can also consider auto-degrading (fewer particles, no links).
